V. LEGAL ARGUMENT

POINT I: MANDAMUS IS THE APPROPRIATE REMEDY

  1. Mandamus is available to compel a federal officer to perform a ministerial duty. Heckler v. Roe, 465 U.S. 312, 314 (1984); In re Cheney, 406 F.3d 723, 729 (D.C. Cir. 2005).

  2. To obtain mandamus, petitioner must demonstrate: (1) a clear right to the relief sought; (2) respondent's duty to act is ministerial and plainly defined; and (3) no other adequate remedy exists. Heckler, 465 U.S. at 314.

  3. All three elements are satisfied here.

  3. The statute uses mandatory language: "shall {{action}}." This leaves no discretion.
