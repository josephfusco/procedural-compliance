Procedural Compliance

How to ask a court to make the government follow the law.

What happened?

A New York State or local agency, official, or court

You cannot use Article 78 to attack a ruling made in a civil or criminal case (CPLR §7801).

  • An official must do something and has not done it.

    Example: A clerk will not file a paper the rules say must be filed.

  • A court or official is acting without the power to act.

    Example: A court keeps going with a case it has no power to hear.

  • An agency made a decision the wrong way.

    Example: An agency denied a license, permit, or benefit without a sound reason.

  • An agency held a hearing, and the proof does not back its ruling.

    Example: A licensing board punished a professional after a hearing.

  • A clerk or court did not follow the rules in your case.

A federal agency, official, or court

  • A federal official or agency must do something for you and has not done it.
  • A federal trial court is doing something it has no power to do.
