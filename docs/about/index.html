About

Plain-language court procedure for people without a lawyer. Free and open source.

Principles.

  1. Follow the written rules.
  2. Check each step before the next.
  3. Put every step in writing.
  4. Pick the path that leaves less to choice.
  5. Cite the law for every request.

Privacy.

This site has no accounts and no forms. It uses Google Analytics to count visits, and Google sets cookies to do that.
