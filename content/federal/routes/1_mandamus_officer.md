---
title: "Mandamus to Compel a Federal Officer"
section: "28 U.S.C. §1361"
order: 1
useWhen: "When an officer or agency of the United States owes you a clear, nondiscretionary duty."
progeny:
  - case: "Heckler v. Ringer"
    cite: "466 U.S. 602, 616 (1984)"
    weight: "root"
    role: "A remedy only after every other avenue of relief is exhausted, and only where the defendant owes the plaintiff a clear nondiscretionary duty."
happened: "A federal official or agency must do something for you and has not done it."
plain: "A federal official or agency must do a certain thing for you and has no choice about it. You have tried every other way to get it done. You ask a federal trial court to order it."
note: "This works only against the federal government. It does not work against a state court, a state clerk, or a state agency. For those, go to state court. In New York State, that is <a class=\"usa-link\" href=\"/new-york/\">Article 78</a>."
---
