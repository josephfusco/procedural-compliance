---
title: "Mandamus to a Lower Court"
section: "28 U.S.C. §1651(a) · Fed. R. App. P. 21"
order: 2
useWhen: "When a district court acts beyond its power and an appeal cannot repair it."
progeny:
  - case: "Cheney v. United States District Court"
    cite: "542 U.S. 367, 380–81 (2004)"
    weight: "root"
    role: "Three conditions: no other adequate means of relief; a clear and indisputable right to the writ; and a court satisfied, in its discretion, that the writ is appropriate."
happened: "A federal trial court is doing something it has no power to do."
plain: "A federal trial court is doing something it has no power to do, and a later appeal cannot fix the harm. You ask the appeals court above it to step in."
note: "File your petition with the clerk of the appeals court. Give a copy to every party and to the trial judge. The appeals court can say no without asking anyone to answer (Fed. R. App. P. 21(a)(1),&nbsp;(b)(1))."
