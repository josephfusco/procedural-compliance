---
title: "Mandamus to Compel"
section: "§7803(1)"
order: 1
useWhen: "When the duty is ministerial."
situation:
  - "A clerk refuses to file or accept a paper the rules require."
  - "An agency fails to issue a determination within the statutory time."
  - "A government body refuses an act the statute makes mandatory in terms."
standard: "The duty must leave no discretion. If the officer weighs facts or chooses among permissible courses, mandamus to review under §7803(3) is the vehicle instead."
authority:
  case: "Klostermann v. Cuomo"
  cite: "61 N.Y.2d 525, 539 (1984)"
  quote: "Mandamus lies to compel the performance of a ministerial act enjoined by law upon a public officer."
happened: "An official must do something and has not done it."
example: "A clerk will not file a paper the rules say must be filed."
plain: "The law says an official must do a certain thing. The official has no choice about it and has not done it. You ask the court to order it done."
---
