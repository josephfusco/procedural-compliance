---
title: "Mandamus to Review"
section: "§7803(3)"
order: 3
useWhen: "When a discretionary determination went off the rails."
situation:
  - "An agency denied a license, permit, or benefit on a record that does not support it."
  - "A determination was made without the procedure the statute requires."
  - "The decision rests on a mistake of law."
standard: "Three grounds, any one sufficient: violation of lawful procedure, error of law, or arbitrary and capricious / abuse of discretion."
authority:
  case: "Pell v. Board of Education"
  cite: "34 N.Y.2d 222, 231 (1974)"
  quote: "Without sound basis in reason and generally taken without regard to the facts."
happened: "An agency made a decision the wrong way."
example: "An agency denied a license, permit, or benefit without a sound reason."
plain: "An agency made a choice it was allowed to make, but made it the wrong way. It skipped a required step, got the law wrong, or had no sound reason. You ask the court to undo it."
---
