---
title: "Substantial Evidence"
section: "§7803(4)"
order: 4
useWhen: "After a hearing on the record."
situation:
  - "An ALJ or hearing officer ruled after sworn testimony."
  - "A licensing board sanctioned a professional after a hearing."
  - "An agency denied benefits after a hearing on the record (not a paper review)."
standard: "Substantial evidence on the record — more than a scintilla, less than a preponderance. CPLR §7804(g) transfers the case to the Appellate Division."
authority:
  case: "300 Gramatan Ave. Assoc. v. State Div. of Human Rights"
  cite: "45 N.Y.2d 176, 180 (1978)"
  quote: "Such relevant proof as a reasonable mind may accept as adequate to support a conclusion or ultimate fact."
happened: "An agency held a hearing, and the proof does not back its ruling."
example: "A licensing board punished a professional after a hearing."
plain: "An agency held a hearing with sworn witnesses and then ruled. You ask the court to check that the proof from the hearing backs up the ruling."
---
