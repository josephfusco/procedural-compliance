---
title: "Prohibition"
section: "§7803(2)"
order: 2
useWhen: "When the tribunal acts beyond jurisdiction."
situation:
  - "A lower court continues in a matter despite a clear lack of jurisdiction."
  - "A judge refuses to recuse where the basis for recusal is mandatory."
  - "An administrative officer is acting outside the authority their statute grants."
standard: "Acting without or in excess of jurisdiction — not merely error within. Extraordinary; disfavored as a routine remedy."
authority:
  case: "La Rocca v. Lane"
  cite: "37 N.Y.2d 575, 579 (1975)"
  quote: "Prohibition is never available merely to correct or prevent trial errors of substantive law or procedure, however grievous."
happened: "A court or officer is acting without the power to act."
example: "A court keeps going with a case it has no power to hear."
plain: "A court or officer is acting without the power to act. You ask the court to make it stop."
