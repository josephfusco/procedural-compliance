---
order: 1
statute: "CPLR §304 · 22 NYCRR §1250.4"
name: "Extra-statutory rejection at intake"
rule: "Filing a verified petition that meets the face requirements of CPLR §304, §7804(c), and 22 NYCRR Part 1250 is a ministerial act. Clerks have no statutory rejection power outside what the rules expressly grant."
breach: "Clerks reject petitions for cover-sheet form, RJI variants, fee-form templates, or formatting demands not enumerated in §1250 — refusing to issue an index number until the petitioner satisfies the rejection."
authority:
  - case: "Brusco v. Braun"
    cite: "84 N.Y.2d 674 (1994)"
  - case: "Klostermann v. Cuomo"
    cite: "61 N.Y.2d 525, 540–41 (1984)"
  - case: "Matter of Council of City of N.Y. v. Bloomberg"
    cite: "6 N.Y.3d 380 (2006)"
why: "The rejection itself blocks creation of the record needed to challenge it. Mandamus against the clerk requires another filing through the same clerk — a circular remedy."
happened: "The clerk would not take your papers."
plain: "A clerk must accept a petition that meets the written rules. Some clerks turn petitions away for reasons the rules do not list. With no case on file, there is no record to show a judge what went wrong."
