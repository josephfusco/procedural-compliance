order: 5
statute: "CPLR §7804(h)"
name: "Triable-issue hearing bypassed"
rule: "If a triable issue of fact is raised, it shall be tried forthwith (CPLR §7804(h)). CPLR §409(b) allows summary determination only to the extent that no triable issues of fact are raised."
breach: "Courts dispose of petitions on the papers without addressing whether triable issues exist, or re-style the petition as a motion and deny it summarily. Affirmance follows without §7804(h) analysis."
authority:
  - source: "CPLR §7804(h)"
  - source: "CPLR §409(b)"
  - case: "Matter of Kickertz v. New York Univ."
    cite: "25 N.Y.3d 942 (2015)"
why: "Once the Appellate Division memorandum decision affirms, the absence of explicit §7804(h) findings is treated as implicit fact-finding favorable to the respondent — leaving no record of the denied hearing to appeal."
happened: "The court decided without a hearing."
plain: "When the two sides disagree about a fact that matters, the law says the court must hold a hearing right away. Courts sometimes decide on the papers alone and skip the hearing. Later courts then treat the facts as if they were decided against you."
