statute: "CPLR §7804(c)"
name: "Service framework confusion"
rule: "Notice of petition and petition must be served at least twenty days before the return date (CPLR §7804(c)). The 20-day rule is sui generis to Article 78 and operates alongside CPLR §306-b's 120-day commencement window — not in place of it."
breach: "Courts and clerks treat ordinary §306-b service as controlling, or conflate §7804(c)'s 20-day return-date rule with §308 personal-service mechanics, dismissing for improper service when the petitioner complied with §7804(c)."
authority:
  - source: "CPLR §7804(c)"
  - source: "CPLR §306-b"
  - case: "Matter of Common Council of Albany v. Town Bd. of Bethlehem"
    cite: "144 A.D.2d 215 (3d Dep't 1988)"
  - case: "Matter of Vetrone v. Mackin"
    cite: "50 A.D.3d 1589 (4th Dep't 2008)"
why: "The two frameworks are in tension and the Court of Appeals has not harmonized them. Petitioners caught between are dismissed where neither rule was actually violated."
happened: "Your case was thrown out over how you served the papers."
plain: "Article 78 has its own rule for giving papers to the other side: at least twenty days before the court date. Courts sometimes use rules meant for other kinds of cases. A case can be thrown out even though the Article 78 rule was met."
