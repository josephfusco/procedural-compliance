order: 7
statute: "CPLR §7804(e)"
name: "Record dependency on the respondent"
rule: "The respondent body or officer shall file with the answer a certified transcript of the record of the proceedings under consideration (CPLR §7804(e)). The petitioner is not required to produce the record."
breach: "Courts dismiss petitions for failure to provide a transcript or insufficient record — particularly where the respondent (a clerk, board, or judge whose action is challenged) is the only entity that can produce it."
authority:
  - source: "CPLR §7804(e)"
  - case: "Matter of Smith v. Town of Plattekill"
    cite: "169 A.D.3d 1265 (3d Dep't 2019)"
  - case: "Matter of Kaur v. New York State Urban Dev. Corp."
    cite: "15 N.Y.3d 235 (2010)"
why: "The petitioner cannot perfect what the respondent withholds. §7804(e)'s shall has no default mechanism analogous to CPLR §3215."
happened: "Your case was thrown out for a missing record."
plain: "The agency or official you are suing must file the full record of what happened. You do not have to. Courts sometimes throw a case out for a missing record that only the other side can supply."
