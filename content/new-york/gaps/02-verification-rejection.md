order: 2
statute: "CPLR §3022"
name: "Verification rejected without §3022 notice"
rule: "CPLR §3022 provides the exclusive remedy for a defective verification: the adverse party must reject the pleading with due diligence and specify the defect. The court has no sua sponte rejection power."
breach: "Clerks and chambers reject pro se petitions for verification form defects — jurat, notarization style, personal-knowledge recitation — without §3022 notice from any adverse party."
authority:
  - source: "CPLR §7804(d)"
  - source: "CPLR §3022"
  - case: "Matter of Miller v. Bd. of Assessors"
    cite: "91 N.Y.2d 82 (1997)"
  - case: "Lentlie v. Egan"
    cite: "94 A.D.2d 839 (3d Dep't 1983), aff'd 61 N.Y.2d 874 (1984)"
why: "§3022 places the burden on the responding party. Unilateral clerk rejection bypasses the statutory safeguard, leaving no adverse party to give notice and no record event for the petitioner to appeal."
happened: "Your paper was rejected over its sworn statement."
plain: "If the sworn statement on a paper has a flaw, the law says the other side must object fast and name the flaw. Clerks and court staff sometimes reject the paper on their own. Then there is no objection on file for you to appeal."
